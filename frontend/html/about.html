About Northeast Institute of Professional Studies (NeIPS)

The Northeast Institute of Professional Studies (NeIPS) is a leading non-governmental organization (NGO) committed to transforming lives through skill development, livelihood creation, and community empowerment in the Northeastern region of India.

Founded by Shri Michael Thongni, a visionary leader and social reformer, NeIPS was born out of a deep realization of the employment crisis faced by the youth in the region. Witnessing the struggles of educated yet unemployed young people, Shri Michael Thongni envisioned an institution that would bridge the gap between potential and opportunity an organization that would empower individuals with the skills, confidence, and resources to shape their own futures.

Since its inception, NeIPS has remained steadfast in its mission to build a skilled, self-reliant, and empowered Northeast. The organization adopts a holistic approach to capacity building combining technical training, soft skills, and entrepreneurial development to prepare individuals for the demands of the modern workforce. NeIPS ensures that its trainees are not only employable but are also equipped to become job creators and leaders of change.

Beyond skill training, NeIPS has expanded its impact through initiatives that promote sustainable livelihoods and inclusive development. The organization actively promotes farmers’ products, develops rural infrastructure through CSR and Rural Haats, empowers persons with disabilities, supports Self-Help Groups (SHGs) through Micro Enterprise Development Programs (MEDP), and provides free schooling for underprivileged children.

Through strategic collaborations with industries, government agencies, and development partners, NeIPS continues to create tangible opportunities for growth and self-reliance. Its community outreach programs, awareness campaigns, and social interventions address critical areas such as education, health, and environmental sustainability, ensuring holistic progress for all.

Driven by the vision and leadership of Shri Michael Thongni, NeIPS stands as a beacon of hope and transformation a movement dedicated to nurturing talent, igniting entrepreneurship, and reshaping the socio-economic landscape of the Northeast. With every program and every life it touches, NeIPS moves closer to its mission of creating a prosperous, skilled, and empowered society where no dream is left unfulfilled.
